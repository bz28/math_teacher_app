# Demo presenter guide — a founder walking a head of education through Veradic

**Who you're pitching.** A head of education at a private school. If they're impressed, they refer you to other private schools — so the whole walkthrough is built to earn that referral. They are silently asking four questions; speak to each one:

1. **"Will my teachers actually adopt this, or is it one more complicated tool?"** — ease is their #1 fear. Thread it the whole way through.
2. **"Can I stand behind this on academic integrity?"** — a private school's reputation rides on it.
3. **"Do I get data on which students and concepts need help?"** — the analytics beat answers this.
4. **"What's the value to my school?"** — the ROI calculator, in their numbers.

The customer demo lives in the **admin dashboard** under **"The pitch"** (`/demo`), or full-screen in **present mode** (`/present`). It's gated to admins, so you open it and screen-share; the prospect never logs in. The pages double as slides — you scroll, you don't click through a deck.

**Total runtime: ~5–7 minutes.** Open on the platform and walk the **flow spine** — the one-loop "day in your teacher's life" — so the head of ed sees it's ONE simple connected workflow, not four tools to roll out. Then dive into one or two of the four deep-dive stories (lead with integrity — the differentiator nobody else has). Each story is a scroll: hero → the real flow → the **★ moment** → proof → payoff. Close on the **school + referral** payoff.

---

## The honesty rules (read once, never break)

We're pre-launch with no production data. The demo is built to be *honest under scrutiny* — keep it that way live:

- **Never quote a production outcome stat.** No "saves 8 hrs/week," no "+15% scores." We don't have them.
- The **accuracy numbers** (0 false flags, catches memorizers, injection-resistant) are **"on our adversarial test set"** — say that phrase.
- The **examples** (the 73% receipt, "Maya Chen," the "6 of 8 struggled") are **illustrative seeded data** — say "this is an example."
- The **ROI calculator is *their* numbers** — "drag these to your class; the math is shown; it's a calculation you control, not a claim."

If a prospect asks "says who?" you should be able to point at the screen. That's the whole design.

## The open — the platform, then the flow spine (`/demo` or `/present`)

Land the hero: **"Snap the homework. The grading and the understanding check run themselves."**

Talk track: *"A math teacher's week is grading at the kitchen table, and never quite knowing who actually understands until the test tells you. Veradic reads the handwritten work, grades it, has a short chat with each student to check they really get it, and hands the teacher back a class they can see at a glance — plus the few who need them."*

**Then walk the flow spine — this is the centerpiece, not a single feature.** Scroll the numbered "A day in your teacher's life" sequence top to bottom and narrate it as ONE connected loop, hitting **ease at every beat** (the green tag on each step is "look how little the teacher does"):

1. **Assign** — *"Start from a worksheet they already have; it becomes a ready-to-assign set, answer key checked."* (ease: their own material)
2. **Students just snap a photo** — *"The student photographs their handwritten work from the phone in their pocket."* (ease: **no typing, no scanner, no new device**)
3. **The AI grades it AND checks they understand** — *"It scores every problem with a receipt, then runs the 3-minute understanding chat — automatically, the teacher doesn't lift a finger."*
4. **You open to a to-do list, not a scoreboard** — pause here, this is the **data beat** for a head of ed: *"The few students who need a human are surfaced first — and underneath, the analytics: exactly which students are slipping and which concepts the whole class is missing. Visibility into learning, not just a wall of averages."* (the struggle-rows shot is on screen)
5. **See the gap, fix it in one click** — *"One click on a weak concept and targeted practice writes itself."*

Land the cap line: **"That's the whole loop — simple enough that your teachers pick it up in a single class."** Then: *"Let me show you the piece nobody else has."* → **The understanding check**.

## Story 1 — The understanding check *(lead here — the differentiator)*

`/golden-set/integrity`

- **Hook:** *"A right answer doesn't prove understanding. A kid can copy, or memorize a worked example. So after they submit, the AI has a calm 3-minute chat."*
- **★ The moment** (scroll to it): *"One question tells understanding from reciting."* Read the memorizer's own words on screen — *"I kind of had that explanation ready… the follow-up I genuinely don't know."* Then the line: **"That one question is the thing no answer key can do."**
- **Proof:** the matrix + stats — *"On our adversarial test set: zero honest students wrongly flagged, and it catches the memorizers."* (Say "test set.")
- **ROI:** drag the calculator. *"Put in your class size, how often you grade, how long a paper takes you. The math is right there — these are your numbers, not my claim."*
- **Kindness beat (if asked about fairness):** the student never sees a verdict; a teacher always makes the call. It's supportive, never an accusation.

## Story 2 — AI grading you can defend

`/golden-set/grading`

- **Hook:** *"Most tools hand you a number and say trust us. We hand you a receipt."*
- **★ The moment:** the itemized **100 − 20 (sign error) − 7 (arithmetic) = 73**, each deduction pinned to the exact step in the student's handwriting. *"When a parent asks why it's a 73, you have the receipt — not an opinion, a breakdown."* (It's an illustrative example.)
- **Payoff beats:** confident grades are pre-approved; you only spend time on the few the AI is unsure about; you override anytime; nothing reaches a student until you publish.

## Story 3 — Problem generation

`/golden-set/generation`

- **Hook:** *"AI generators hand you a clean-looking problem with a quietly-wrong answer key. Ours doesn't trust its own first pass."*
- **★ The moment:** the generated problem beside its **independent hand re-derivation** — both landing on the same answer (41°). *"It grades its own work before it ever reaches your students. Only problems that survive the check land in your review queue."*

---

## Story 4 — Your teaching week

`/golden-set/teacher-day`

- **Hook:** *"Open the app to a to-do list, not a scoreboard — the students who need you, surfaced first, across all your courses."*
- **★ The moment:** *"Seeing the problem and fixing it are the same click."* The class is ranked by where it's stuck ("6 of 8 missed distributing a negative" — an example) → one click → the AI writes ten fresh practice problems on exactly that concept. *"Every other tool stops at the dashboard. This turns it into the fix."*

## The close — for the school, and the referral

Back to `/demo`, land the school payoff (it speaks to the leader, not one teacher): **every teacher gets their evenings back and adopts it in a single class · integrity you can put your name to and defend to any parent · no student quietly falls behind, because you see where every one of them is.**

Then the warm referral note — say it plainly: *"Honestly, this is what I'd want every school you know to have. If it earns it, an introduction to the schools in your circle would mean the world."* That line is on screen; let it sit.
