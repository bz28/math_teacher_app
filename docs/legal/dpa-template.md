# Data Privacy Agreement (DPA) — Template

**Vendor:** Veradic LLC ("Vendor")
**District / Local Education Agency:** _______________________ ("LEA")
**Effective Date:** _______________________

This Data Privacy Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between the Vendor and the LEA and governs the handling of Student Data and other Personal Information processed in connection with the Vendor's services ("Services"). This template is provided as a starting point. Where the LEA uses a standard template such as the National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) or a state-specific addendum (e.g., New York Education Law §2-d, California SOPIPA, Illinois SOPPA), the Vendor will execute the LEA's preferred template.

---

## 1. Definitions

**"Student Data"** means any Personally Identifiable Information collected, stored, or processed in connection with the Services on behalf of the LEA, including but not limited to student name, contact information, grade level, school enrollment, coursework, assessments, and any other information directly or indirectly identifying a student.

**"Personal Information"** means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with a particular individual, including LEA employees, contractors, and parents/guardians.

**"Education Record"** has the meaning set forth in the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act ("FERPA"), 20 U.S.C. § 1232g.

**"Security Incident"** means an unauthorized acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of Student Data that compromises the security, confidentiality, or integrity of such data.

---

## 2. Purpose and Permitted Use

The Vendor processes Student Data and Personal Information solely to provide the Services to the LEA. The Vendor will not:

1. Sell or rent Student Data;
2. Use Student Data for advertising, marketing, or profiling;
3. Use Student Data to train, develop, or improve generative AI models, including the Vendor's own models or those of third parties; or
4. Disclose Student Data to any third party except as permitted under this DPA.

---

## 3. Vendor as a School Official

For purposes of FERPA, the Vendor acts as a "school official" with a "legitimate educational interest" under 34 C.F.R. § 99.31(a)(1) when processing Student Data on behalf of the LEA. The Vendor performs services that the LEA would otherwise perform itself, is under the direct control of the LEA with respect to the use and maintenance of Education Records, and is subject to the requirements of FERPA governing the use and re-disclosure of Personally Identifiable Information from Education Records.

---

## 4. Confidentiality and Access Controls

The Vendor will:

1. Restrict access to Student Data to those personnel with a need to know in connection with the Services;
2. Require all personnel with access to Student Data to be bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations;
3. Implement role-based access controls to prevent unauthorized access; and
4. Maintain commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Student Data.

---

## 5. Security

The Vendor maintains a written information security program that includes safeguards reasonably designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Student Data, including:

1. Encryption of Student Data in transit using current TLS standards;
2. Encryption of Student Data at rest using industry-standard encryption provided by the Vendor's cloud infrastructure;
3. Authentication controls including password protection and account-lockout protections;
4. Role-based access controls and audit logging of system activity;
5. Industry-standard application security practices including input validation and protection against common web vulnerabilities; and
6. Ongoing review and improvement of the security program.

Additional security detail is available in the Vendor's Trust & Security disclosure at https://veradicai.com/trust.

---

## 6. Security Incident Notification

In the event of a confirmed Security Incident affecting LEA Student Data, the Vendor will:

1. Notify the LEA in writing within **seventy-two (72) hours** of confirmation of the incident;
2. Provide a written summary describing the nature of the incident, the categories of data affected, the approximate number of affected individuals if known, and any remediation steps taken or planned;
3. Cooperate with the LEA in any required notifications to affected individuals or regulators; and
4. Provide updates as additional information becomes available.

---

## 7. Subprocessors

The Vendor may engage subprocessors to provide portions of the Services (for example, cloud infrastructure providers, AI service providers used to generate tutoring content, and payment processors). A current list of subprocessors is available on request. The Vendor remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subprocessors with respect to Student Data.

---

## 8. Data Subject Rights and Requests

The Vendor will provide reasonable assistance to the LEA in responding to requests from parents, guardians, eligible students, or other data subjects regarding their Personal Information, including requests for access, correction, deletion, or portability, in accordance with applicable law.

To request export or deletion of Student Data, the LEA may contact support@veradicai.com.

---

## 9. Data Retention and Destruction

The Vendor will retain Student Data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services or as required by applicable law. Upon termination of the Services or upon written request from the LEA, the Vendor will return or destroy Student Data within a commercially reasonable timeframe, except as required to be retained for legal, regulatory, or legitimate business purposes.

---

## 10. Audits

Upon reasonable written request and no more than once per year, the LEA may request information from the Vendor regarding the Vendor's compliance with this DPA. The Vendor will provide reasonable responses to such inquiries.

---

## 11. Term and Termination

This DPA takes effect on the Effective Date and remains in effect for the duration of the Services Agreement between the parties. Termination of the Services Agreement terminates this DPA, except that obligations relating to confidentiality, security, and the return or destruction of Student Data survive termination.

---

## 12. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction of the LEA, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. State-specific addenda may modify this provision as required by applicable state law.

---

## 13. Order of Precedence

If this DPA conflicts with the Services Agreement, this DPA controls with respect to the handling of Student Data. If the LEA provides an executed state-specific addendum or NDPA exhibit, that document controls with respect to its specific provisions.
