# Accessibility Statement

**Version:** 1.0
**Last reviewed:** May 23, 2026

Veradic is committed to making its platform usable by everyone, including students, teachers, and administrators who rely on assistive technology. This statement describes our current accessibility posture, the standards we target, and how to reach us with feedback or accommodation requests.

---

## Target Standards

Veradic is designed with the goal of conforming to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA**, the standard most commonly referenced in U.S. education-sector accessibility requirements and consistent with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act.

Veradic also aims to operate in compliance with the **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)** and to avoid interfering with accommodations provided under the **Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA)**, including those documented in student IEP and 504 plans.

---

## Current Status

Veradic is built on standard accessible web technology and includes the following:

- Semantic HTML structure across pages
- Keyboard navigation with visible focus indication and managed focus in modal dialogs
- Form labels linked to inputs, with error messages presented in association with the input
- ARIA attributes where required to communicate state to assistive technology
- Image alternative text where images convey meaning

Veradic is engaged in continuing accessibility work, including:

- Expanding alternative-text coverage across content surfaces
- Validating color contrast ratios against WCAG 2.1 AA thresholds
- Testing with screen readers and keyboard-only navigation across teacher and student workflows
- Identifying and remediating any timed interactions to support accommodations under student IEPs and 504 plans

A formal **Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT 2.4 INT)** documenting Veradic's conformance against each WCAG 2.1 AA criterion is available on request.

---

## Known Limitations

As of the date of this statement, Veradic acknowledges the following areas where conformance work is ongoing:

- Alternative text coverage is not yet exhaustive across all visual content
- Color contrast has not been independently validated against WCAG 2.1 AA across all design tokens
- Screen-reader compatibility testing has not been completed across all workflows
- Skip-navigation links are not yet implemented on all pages

Veradic prioritizes accessibility improvements based on user feedback, audit findings, and regulatory requirements.

---

## Assistive Technology Compatibility

Veradic does not block, interfere with, or override standard assistive technology including screen readers, voice control, screen magnification, or alternative input devices. The platform is delivered through a standards-compliant web browser, which allows users to apply their own preferred assistive technology configurations.

---

## Accommodations and Feedback

Veradic welcomes feedback on accessibility, including reports of barriers encountered while using the platform and requests for accommodations.

To request an accommodation, report an accessibility barrier, or request a copy of Veradic's VPAT or supporting accessibility documentation:

**Email:** support@veradicai.com

We aim to respond to accessibility inquiries within **5 business days** and to engage in good-faith efforts to address reported barriers.
