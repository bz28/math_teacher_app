# Curriculum Alignment Statement

**Version:** 1.0
**Last reviewed:** May 23, 2026

This statement summarizes Veradic's approach to curriculum alignment and is provided to school districts as part of procurement and curriculum-review processes.

---

## Standards Veradic Aligns With

Veradic content is designed to align with widely-adopted U.S. K-12 academic standards, including:

- **Common Core State Standards (CCSS) for Mathematics** — grade-level standards for K-12 mathematics, focusing on conceptual understanding, procedural fluency, and application
- **Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)** — three-dimensional science standards combining disciplinary core ideas, science and engineering practices, and crosscutting concepts
- **State-adopted variants** of the above, including Pennsylvania's STEELS standards (Science, Technology, Engineering, Environmental Literacy, Sustainability) and other state-specific frameworks

Veradic supports the subject areas of mathematics, physics, and chemistry across middle-school, high-school, and introductory-college levels.

---

## How Alignment Is Approached

Veradic's problem generation, tutoring, and feedback systems are designed to support the practices identified in the standards above — including problem-solving, modeling, reasoning, communication, and the application of disciplinary core ideas. Content is reviewed for academic accuracy and grade-level appropriateness before deployment.

Veradic does not replace district-adopted curriculum. It is positioned as a complementary tool that supports teachers in delivering, reinforcing, and assessing standards-aligned instruction.

---

## Detailed Mapping

A detailed mapping of Veradic's problem templates, tutoring scenarios, and content modules to specific standard codes (e.g., CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.8.EE.A.1, HS-PS1-1) is available on request as part of curriculum-committee due-diligence.

Districts requiring alignment to a specific state framework, district-adopted scope and sequence, or course-specific learning objectives may request a tailored alignment review.

---

## Updates

Veradic's curriculum alignment is reviewed periodically and updated as standards evolve and as the platform's content coverage expands. Material updates will be reflected in this statement.
