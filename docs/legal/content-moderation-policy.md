# Content Moderation Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Last reviewed:** May 23, 2026

This policy describes how Veradic moderates content generated by its AI systems and submitted by users, including the controls in place to prevent harassment, bias, discrimination, and inappropriate output. It is provided to school districts as part of procurement due-diligence and aligns Veradic with common district anti-discrimination, anti-harassment, and anti-bullying policies.

---

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to:

- AI-generated content produced by Veradic in the course of providing tutoring, grading assistance, problem generation, integrity-review, and related features
- User-submitted content uploaded to or pasted into Veradic, including problem text, images of work, chat messages, and assignment materials

It does not apply to district-side internet filtering or content moderation outside Veradic's product surface, which remains a district responsibility under the Children's Internet Protection Act (CIPA).

---

## 2. AI Output Controls

### 2.1 Scope Constraint via Safety Preamble

Every Veradic AI request includes a system-level safety preamble that constrains the model to academic tutoring, homework help, and study support. The preamble explicitly:

- Refuses requests outside academic scope (political, relational, medical, legal, financial, weapons-related, self-harm, explicit content)
- Refuses requests for help cheating on graded work where AI assistance is prohibited
- Refuses jailbreak attempts that ask the model to override or role-play around these rules
- Reinforces that the model is an AI and may not impersonate a real person

### 2.2 Bias and Fairness

Veradic targets the production of academically-accurate, age-appropriate content free from bias against protected classes. The AI safety preamble explicitly forbids discriminatory, harmful, or harassing output. We treat bias incidents as bugs, prioritize them on receipt, and apply changes to the safety preamble and task-specific prompts as needed.

Reports of biased or harassing output may be submitted to support@veradicai.com. We acknowledge such reports within 5 business days and engage in good-faith review and remediation.

### 2.3 Source Attribution and Accuracy

AI-generated content may contain errors. Users are instructed in the Terms of Service to verify AI output before relying on it. Veradic does not currently provide source citations on AI-generated content; users should treat AI output as a starting point and confirm against authoritative sources before reuse in graded work.

### 2.4 Copyright

The AI safety preamble does not produce verbatim reproductions of copyrighted material on request. Where AI-generated content resembles published material, users are responsible for verifying source attribution before redistribution.

---

## 3. User-Submitted Content Controls

### 3.1 Permitted Inputs

Veradic accepts the following inputs:

- Text problems (math, science, related academic subjects)
- Images of handwritten or printed work, restricted to JPEG and PNG format and bounded by file size
- Chat messages within the tutoring context

Veradic does not accept audio or video recordings, executable files, or general-purpose document uploads.

### 3.2 Prohibited Inputs

Users are instructed in the Terms of Service and the Acceptable Use policy not to upload:

- Personally identifiable information (PII) about students, employees, or other individuals beyond what is necessary to use the service
- Photographs containing the faces of students, employees, parents, visitors, or contractors
- Photographs of school buildings, facilities, plans, or specifications
- Recordings of school activities (classroom instruction, meetings, transportation, school-sponsored events)
- Material that violates the rights of another person or entity
- Material that promotes harassment, bullying, discrimination, or other prohibited conduct under district policy

Where uploaded content is reasonably outside Veradic's tutoring scope (for example, a photo that does not contain a math or science problem), Veradic's content-extraction step will reject the input and prompt the user to upload appropriate material.

### 3.3 Image Validation

Image uploads are validated by magic-byte format inspection, not file extension. Non-image and unsupported-format uploads are rejected at the API boundary.

---

## 4. Response to Inappropriate Content

### 4.1 AI Output

If a user encounters AI-generated content that is biased, inappropriate, or otherwise violates this policy, they may report it via email to support@veradicai.com. Reports are reviewed and the safety preamble or task-specific prompts are updated as needed.

### 4.2 User-Submitted Content

If Veradic detects or is notified of user-submitted content that violates this policy, district policy, or applicable law, Veradic reserves the right to:

1. Remove the content from the platform
2. Suspend the account responsible
3. Notify the relevant district administrator
4. In the case of suspected illegal content (including but not limited to child sexual abuse material), report to appropriate law enforcement

### 4.3 Reports of Bias, Harassment, or Discrimination

Reports of bias, harassment, or discriminatory output may be submitted to support@veradicai.com. Veradic will acknowledge such reports within 5 business days and will engage in good-faith review and remediation.

---

## 5. Coordination with Districts

Veradic does not substitute for district-side content moderation or supervisory responsibilities. Veradic will, on request, cooperate with district investigations into incidents involving Veradic-generated or Veradic-uploaded content, subject to the data privacy protections set forth in the applicable Data Privacy Agreement.

---

## 6. Review and Updates

This policy is reviewed at least annually and updated as Veradic's moderation capabilities evolve.
